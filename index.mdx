The portable trust harness.

Proofable keeps agent identity, authority, context, connections, limits, and current proof together across models and runtimes. Your existing framework runs the work. Proofable carries the trust state the next tool needs.

## Get started

Add `https://mcp.proofable.me/mcp` to your AI client, click **Connect**, then ask:

> Show my Proofable profile and current proofs.

Use the [one-click installer](https://proofable.me/install) or follow the [MCP setup guide](./mcp/setup).
