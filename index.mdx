Proofable is the trust harness for AI. Every agent gets its own identity, the context it needs, clear limits on what it can do, and a signed proof of what it did that anyone can verify offline. People and businesses verify once and reuse that proof anywhere. All of it travels with you across models, tools and MCP clients.

Add it to your AI tool, or build it into your product. Free with 500 credits a month.
