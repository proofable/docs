Proofable checks every AI agent tool call against limits you set, blocks the calls you did not allow, and signs a receipt you can verify offline. Free to start with 500 credits a month.

```bash
npx -y @proofable/sdk setup
```

Adds Proofable to the AI tools on your machine. Your client opens the sign-in the first time you use it.
