Anyone can check that proof offline. People and businesses verify once and reuse it anywhere, across models, tools and MCP clients. Free with 500 credits a month.

```bash
npx -y @proofable/sdk setup
```

Adds Proofable to the AI tools on your machine. Your client opens the sign-in the first time you use it.
