Proofable is the trust harness for AI. It puts agents on real work without your keys: each one gets a permission for its job, covering the tools it can use, what it can spend on each payment, and when its access ends. Every action is checked before it runs, risky steps can wait for your approval, and every run leaves a signed proof anyone can verify offline.

People can also prove once that they are human or own a domain, and reuse that proof at every later check.

Start in your AI tool, or build it into your product. Free with 500 credits a month.
