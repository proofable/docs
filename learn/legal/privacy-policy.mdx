This Privacy Policy explains how NEUS Network, Inc. ("Proofable," "we," "us," or "our") handles personal information when you use the Proofable website, application, APIs, hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) service, the Proofable tools an MCP client calls, agent and job execution, verification services, and related products (collectively, the "Service").

Those services have their own privacy practices.

## What the Proofable Tools Process

An MCP client such as ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor or Codex calls the Proofable MCP service on your behalf. Some tools return only public information and work without an account. The rest run against your signed-in profile and process the information below. This section states what each tool takes in, what it returns, and where the information goes, so you can see the tool surface as it exists.

### Tools that run without an account

| Tool | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read the profile context | Profile identifier, or the signed-in profile | Read-only profile summary, proof index, agent list, permissions and supported checks |
| Read the verifier catalog | None, or a check ID | Supported check definitions with the inputs and networks each requires |
| Check for existing proofs | Wallet address, chain and a list of required checks | Pass or fail only. No proof bodies, records or content are returned |
| Resolve a verification request | Wallet address, check IDs and request context | Reusable qualifying proofs when they exist, or the next secure step to complete |

## Google User Data

Signing in with Google and connecting Google as an agent capability are separate choices:

- **Google sign-in** requests only your Google account identifier and email address (`openid` and `email`) to authenticate you and link the sign-in method to your Proofable account.
- **Google connection** (for example Gmail, Google Drive, or Google Calendar) is made through Composio, our connection provider. Google's consent screen shows the exact access requested. Proofable uses the connection only when you ask an assistant or agent to use it.

Proofable handles Google user data as follows:

- **Access:** Proofable accesses data only through the permissions you approve and only to provide the user-facing feature you request.
- **Use:** Retrieved data is used to answer your request, perform the tool action, and show or save the result in your private Proofable session or proof when that feature is enabled.
- **Storage:** On the Composio path, Composio stores and refreshes the connection tokens and Proofable stores a reference to your connection. When you grant a connection to an agent so the agent can keep calling the provider, Proofable instead receives the provider's refresh or access token and stores it encrypted in your Vault as a server-written credential record. Retrieved content may be retained in a private chat, job, or execution proof if it forms part of that record.
- **Sharing:** Data may be processed by Composio, the Proofable hosting stack and, when needed to answer your request, by the hosted AI provider or the AI provider you selected. It is not shared for advertising or unrelated purposes.
- **AI training:** Proofable does not use raw or derived Google Workspace data to train or improve a generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model.
- **Hosted model isolation:** When you use Proofable-hosted assistant inference (the Default and Advanced tiers), Proofable sends the model request through RedPill's confidential-computing gateway. Proofable requires a verified serving path and, for each completed model call, checks the gateway identity and the signed receipt's exact request and response hashes. When the gateway forwards to a separate model host, Proofable also checks the cited serving-session record, its evidence digest, claims, and channel binding. The chat shows **TEE verified** only when every model call that contributed to the answer passed the checks that apply to its serving path. This verifies the recorded processing path; it does not mean the browser-to-model request is end-to-end encrypted. Proofable-side retention of the conversation or proof is separate and described above. When you supply your own AI provider key (bring-your-own-key), Proofable sends your request to the provider you selected. The same **TEE verified** checks can apply when that provider is RedPill's verified gateway; they do not apply to other bring-your-own-key providers. The selected provider's terms and privacy policy govern its processing.
- **Control:** You can disconnect Google in Proofable. Proofable removes the local connection and attempts provider-side revocation. You can also revoke access from your [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

## Third-Party AI Providers

Proofable offers two inference paths with different data-isolation guarantees.

**Proofable-hosted inference (Default and Advanced tiers).** When you use the included Proofable assistant without supplying your own key, Proofable sends the model request through RedPill's confidential-computing gateway and requires verified serving. For every completed model call, Proofable can check the gateway's hardware-backed identity, measured software, key-release chain, and TLS channel, plus the exact request and response hashes in its signed receipt. When the gateway forwards to a separate model host, Proofable also checks the cited serving-session record, its evidence digest, claims, and channel binding. A **TEE verified** label means all model calls that contributed to that answer passed the checks that apply to their serving paths. The session check relies on the approved gateway's attested verification result; Proofable does not separately rerun every model host's provider-specific attestation process. The checks do not independently prove exclusive custody of the gateway's private keys. The label does not claim end-to-end encryption or prove how a model developer handles data outside this serving path. Proofable-side retention of the conversation or proof is separate and described above.

**Bring-your-own-key inference.** When you supply your own AI provider key, Proofable sends your request to the provider you selected, and that provider's terms and privacy policy apply. A RedPill key can use the same verified gateway and **TEE verified** checks described above. Other bring-your-own-key providers do not receive that label. If you connect Google Workspace and then select a bring-your-own-key provider for a turn that uses Google data, you direct the transfer of that data to the provider, and you are responsible for confirming the provider's terms permit your use.

The following third-party AI providers may be available as bring-your-own-key integrations, each governed by the provider's own terms: OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Groq, RedPill, Ollama Cloud, NEAR AI, Alibaba DashScope, Featherless AI, Mistral, Together, xAI, NVIDIA NIM, and Fireworks AI. Proofable does not control how a provider processes data you send with your own key. The Proofable-hosted path uses RedPill as its sole platform-managed provider and requires the verified serving path described above.

## Retention and Deletion

We keep information only for the period needed for the purposes described above:

- account, profile, private proof, connection, chat, session receipt, and agent records remain until you delete or revoke them, delete your profile, or they are no longer needed;
- a saved secret remains until you replace it under the same name, revoke it, or delete your profile. Replacing a secret revokes its prior value immediately;
- a saved tool, skill or agent instruction remains until you update or delete it, or delete your profile;
- a remote MCP connection remains until you remove it or delete your profile;
- a comment or vote remains with the proof it attaches to until you clear it or the proof is deleted;
- browser session content, screenshots and run artifacts from a job remain with that job's output until you delete the job or your profile;
- contact requests, support messages and security reports remain for the period needed to answer them and keep a record of the request;
- connected-provider credentials remain until you disconnect, reconnect and replace them, the provider invalidates them, you revoke the agent grant that created them, or you delete your profile;
- authentication and OAuth transaction data expires after the applicable security period;
- service, security, and diagnostic records remain for the period needed to operate, secure, and audit the Service;
- public and third-party source records are kept only as part of the proof that used them, and are governed by the proof's own retention above. Public blockchain and other public network records are outside our control and may never be erasable; and
- payment, dispute, and compliance records remain as required by tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, and other laws.

Where a record is kept because it is still needed to operate, secure, or audit the Service rather than because you hold an active account, we remove it within 24 months of your account closing, unless a shorter period applies above or a longer one is required by law. Records preserved by law, by an open dispute, or on a public network we cannot alter are outside that window.

When you delete your profile, Proofable deletes the profile record and revokes associated proofs from Proofable-controlled access where technically possible. We may retain limited records when required by law, needed to protect the Service, or present on a public network that Proofable cannot alter.
