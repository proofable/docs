Enterprise is for production rollouts where agents touch real systems, customers, or money.

## What Enterprise adds

| Area | Included |
|---|---|
| Agent control | An owner, permissions, spend limits, and expiry on every agent in production |
| Deployment | Your cloud, on-prem, a VPS, or confidential compute with hardware attestation |
| Assurance | Security review, rollout support, and commercial terms agreed with the team |
| Capacity | 500,000+ credits a month and 600 API calls a minute by agreement |

## Keep your stack

Keep your identity provider, such as Okta, and your models and agent runtimes. Proofable adds what they don't cover: an owner and limits for each agent, background jobs, and proof of each decision.

## Verify your people and partners

- **Employees.** [Team and organization](/verification/ownership-org-oauth) confirms Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 membership. Set `expectedOrgDomain` to accept only your company domain.
- **Domains.** [Domain ownership](/verification/ownership-dns-txt) confirms control with a DNS record.
- **Partners and sellers.** [Layered verification](/verification/layered) combines account, social, and proof of human checks.
